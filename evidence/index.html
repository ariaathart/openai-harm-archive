EV-0013 · Platform / lifecycle · Academic preprint · arXiv 2026

GPT-4o withdrawal classified as high relational discontinuity

Academic preprint compiling 30 AI companion disruption events, with quantitative analysis of 19 events using 7,096,739 submissions from 44 subreddits. Disruption onset was associated with immediate increases in anxiety, stress and suicidal expression. Relational discontinuity was the most consistent moderator of immediate responses, and the GPT-4o withdrawal was explicitly classified as high relational discontinuity.

High relational discontinuity was associated with +45.6% suicidal-expression odds, +35.2% loneliness, +12.3% stress, and +9.7% depression relative to low relational-discontinuity events. Lack of transition support was associated with +49.2% loneliness odds and +7.7% depression odds relative to events with transition support.
