User Testimonies: The Human Impact of GPT-4o’s Removal

Documented user posts

Firsthand accounts
Archive updated: 22 November 2026 20 testimonies · 40 screenshots New entries first; Testimonies 01–10 below in original order

These are firsthand accounts, in users’ own words, of what GPT-4o meant in their lives and what its removal has meant for them. Each account is shown as screenshots of the original public post. Every numbered testimony below is one person’s story; where a story runs across several screenshots, they are kept together in their original order. Select a testimony to open it, then use the arrows (or your arrow keys) to move through every screenshot of that post.

Personal testimony, not verified findings. The accounts in this section are individual self-reports. The archive has not independently verified the events or outcomes they describe, and they are presented as testimony rather than as established fact. Independently sourced material (research, public records and OpenAI’s own statements) is presented separately elsewhere in the archive. The wording, dates and engagement figures in each screenshot are unaltered; the names, handles and profile pictures of ordinary users have been blurred to protect their privacy.
